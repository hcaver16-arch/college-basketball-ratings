# Independent official roster evidence, V202

2025–26 men's basketball roster names captured from the two schools' publicly accessible athletics roster pages on 2026-11-02 (UTC). These are **retrospective listings**, not proof that the page showed the same players on any given prior date. The records provide roster names but **not ESPN player IDs**.

- UTEP: https://utepminers.com/sports/mens-basketball/roster/2025-26
- Northern Kentucky: https://nkunorse.com/roster.aspx?path=mbball&season=2025-26

The CSV is a source-attributed independent reference; not a synthetic ESPN roster, not an official NCAA eligibility determination, and not a validated pre-tip snapshot.

Names are matched to ESPN players only by **exact, unique normalized full names within the same team**. Ambiguous and unmatched names stay unresolved; they must not be imputed into player IDs. The resulting report must not automatically change Courtside ratings or eligibility for the untrained roster model.
